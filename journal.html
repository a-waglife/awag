Bhairav at 4am

The science and the silence of the pre-dawn raga

Raga Bhairav belongs to the brahma muhurta — 3:30 to 5:30am, before the sun but after deep night. Its defining note is komal Dha, ratio 8:5. Plotted as a Lissajous figure, that ratio draws an asymmetric three-lobe closed curve. That curve becomes the garment. Not a symbol of the raga — the raga itself, in form.

The Geometry of Kashi

Why ghat steps are the most honest map of existence

The ghats of Varanasi descend to the Ganga at a specific angle, worn smooth by three thousand years of feet. Viewed from above: parallel lines of decreasing width converging toward water. The progressive dissolution of form as it approaches the formless. A diagram of the human condition, carved in stone.
