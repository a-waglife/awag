Global Organic Textile Standard. The full supply chain — from cotton field to finished garment — certified organic. No pesticides at source. No toxic chemistry in process.

Every print uses water-based inks. No PVC, no solvent discharge. The ink bonds with the cotton fibre rather than sitting on top of it. The print ages with the garment — it does not crack, it does not peel.
